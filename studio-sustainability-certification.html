Positioning

The facility has a footprint too.

A studio's physical infrastructure — sound stages, lighting circuits, generators, water systems, waste contracts, green space, and workforce policies — creates a sustainability performance that exists independently of any single production shooting there. Climactix's Studio Sustainability Certification assesses that facility-level performance, so studio landlords and operators can measure, evidence and improve it directly.

Certification Levels

Climactix Studio Certification.

These are Climactix Global certification levels, using the same tier structure as Climactix's production-level certification. They are issued following independent review of a studio's submitted evidence — not from self-reported answers alone — and do not imply BAFTA albert or any government accreditation.

Bronze
40+ / 100

  • Basic waste segregation

  • + Sustainability targets & monitoring
  • + ≥3 segregated waste streams
Formalise a sustainability policy with targets and regular review.
Gold
70+ / 100
Active reduction
  • + ≥25% on-site or tariff renewables
  • + Low-VOC / certified timber sourcing
Reach a documented renewable electricity share and green space plan.
Platinum
82+ / 100
Facility-wide performance
  • + Majority renewable electricity
  • + Biodiversity / nature investment

Evidence-Based Credential

We don't certify claims. We assess evidence.

Climactix reviews the documentation behind a studio's KPI answers — not the answers alone.

  • Utility meter records (electricity, gas, water)
  • Renewable tariff / REGO certificates
  • Waste contractor volume & recycling reports
  • Timber certification (FSC / PEFC / SFI)
  • Sustainable procurement policy
  • Climate Change Risk Assessment & mitigation plan
  • EDI policy & training records
  • Mental Health First Aider register
  • Green space / biodiversity survey
  • Community investment records
  • Approved third-party supplier lists
  • Independent verification sign-off

Assessment Process

Self-assessment, then independent review.

The Round 4 methodology is explicit about this sequence: a self-assessed score, followed by independent review of the underlying evidence.

Self-Assessment
  • Answer each KPI
  • Provide a short narrative per answer
  • Live composite score
  • Identify improvement areas
Evidence Submission
  • Attach documented evidence
  • Complete mandatory data section
  • Internal or third-party data verification
Independent Assessment
  • Evidence reviewed against answers
  • Grade & certification level issued
  • Report with improvement areas

Climactix does not claim endorsement, accreditation, partnership, or certification by BAFTA albert.
