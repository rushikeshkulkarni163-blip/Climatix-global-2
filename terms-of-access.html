Acceptable use & data protection policy

Rules governing unauthorized access and data infringement

01

Scope & definitions

This policy governs access to and use of the Climactix Global platform, including Risk OS, the Internal Analyst System, the Evidence Intelligence Agent, all associated APIs, databases, and exported reports (collectively, the "Platform"). It applies to every enterprise user, investor user, Climactix Global employee, contractor, and any other individual granted a credential to the Platform (collectively, "Authorized Users").

Definitions

  • Client Data — any information a company submits to the Platform: assessment answers, uploaded evidence, facility records, and identifying company information.
  • Derived Intelligence — any output computed by the Platform from Client Data: C-LAYER scores, Evidence Confidence scores, extracted claims and metrics, contradiction flags, greenwashing risk signals, and generated reports.
  • Confidential Data — Client Data and Derived Intelligence together, regardless of the format or system in which it is held.
  • Authorized Scope — the specific companies, assessments, and functions a given Authorized User's role and credentials permit them to access, as enforced by the Platform's access-control rules.
02

Unauthorized access

Access to the Platform is granted strictly for the purpose associated with an Authorized User's role — submitting or managing an enterprise's own assessment, reviewing assessments as Climactix staff, or evaluating portfolio companies as an investor. The following are prohibited without prior written authorization from Climactix Global:

  1. Accessing, or attempting to access, any company, assessment, or record outside your Authorized Scope.
  2. Sharing your login credentials, session, or authentication token with any other person, or using another individual's credentials.
  3. Probing, scanning, or testing the Platform's access controls, authentication mechanisms, or Firestore security rules, including through automated tools.
  4. Attempting to elevate privileges, impersonate another Authorized User, or bypass role-based access restrictions by any technical or procedural means.
  5. Accessing the Platform after your authorization has been suspended, revoked, or has expired.
  6. Introducing malicious code, credentials-harvesting tools, or unauthorized integrations against the Platform's APIs or databases.
Consequences of unauthorized access

Unauthorized access will result in immediate revocation of credentials, and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or engagement, civil liability for resulting damages, and referral to law enforcement under applicable computer misuse and cybersecurity laws in the relevant jurisdiction.

03

Data infringement & misuse

Confidential Data is provided to Authorized Users solely to perform their authorized function on the Platform. The following rules apply to all Confidential Data, whether viewed on-screen, exported as a report, or accessed via API:

RuleApplies to
No copying, downloading, or exporting Confidential Data outside the Platform's own sanctioned export/report functions.All Authorized Users
No redistribution, publication, or disclosure of Confidential Data to any third party without the company's and Climactix Global's written consent.All Authorized Users
No use of one client's Confidential Data to inform, benchmark, or advantage a different client or a competing engagement.Staff & investors
No reverse-engineering, extraction, or replication of the C-LAYER scoring methodology, the Evidence Intelligence Agent's models, or any underlying algorithm.All Authorized Users
No retention of Confidential Data beyond the period required for the authorized function, and prompt deletion of any local copies once that function is complete.Staff
No alteration of an assessment's underlying answers or evidence on a company's behalf; review actions and score overrides must go through the Platform's audited review workflow.Staff

All Derived Intelligence remains the intellectual property of Climactix Global. Client Data remains the property of the submitting company, licensed to Climactix Global solely for the purpose of producing Derived Intelligence on that company's behalf.

Breach notification

Any Authorized User who becomes aware of unauthorized disclosure, loss, or misuse of Confidential Data must report it immediately under Section 6, regardless of how the exposure occurred.

04

Monitoring & audit

All sign-ins, data access, review actions, score overrides, and report generation on the Platform are logged against the Authorized User's identity and retained for audit purposes. Climactix Global reserves the right to review these logs at any time to investigate suspected unauthorized access or data misuse, without prior notice to the Authorized User under review.

05

Enforcement

Violations of this policy are assessed by Climactix Global's platform administrators and, where warranted, escalated to legal and compliance teams. Enforcement may include, proportionate to the violation:

  • Immediate suspension or revocation of Platform access.
  • Disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, contract, or client relationship.
  • Civil claims for damages arising from misuse or disclosure of Confidential Data.
  • Referral to law enforcement or regulatory authorities where the conduct may constitute a criminal offense.
06

Reporting violations

Suspected unauthorized access, data misuse, or any other violation of this policy should be reported to your platform administrator as soon as it is discovered. Do not attempt to independently investigate, reproduce, or remediate a suspected breach before reporting it.

07

Acknowledgement

By signing in to the Platform, an Authorized User acknowledges that they have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this policy for the duration of their access.
