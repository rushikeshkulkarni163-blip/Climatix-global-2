Positioning

Sustainability that can be measured.

Film production creates a measurable environmental footprint through travel, energy, fuel, materials, accommodation, catering, water, waste and production logistics. Climactix helps production houses establish a baseline, measure environmental impact, implement reduction measures and produce an evidence-backed sustainability assessment.

01
Measure
Log activity data across every production phase and convert it into CO₂e, water, and waste using a multi-country emission-factor database.
02
Reduce
Identify reduction opportunities — renewable power, waste diversion, low-carbon transport — against a running baseline.
03
Verify
Supporting evidence — invoices, bills, logs, declarations — is reviewed against every logged activity before scoring.
04
Certify
Productions clearing a tier's minimum score and mandatory criteria receive a signed, independently verifiable certificate.
Climactix Sustainable Production Framework™

From registration to a verifiable certificate.

Ten stages carry a production from initial registration through to a certified, evidence-backed impact report.

01
Production Registration
Register the production, production company, country, film type, crew and cast size, and shoot dates.
02
Pre-Production Assessment
A structured sustainability questionnaire covering location, travel planning, procurement, energy planning, and waste planning.
03
Environmental Baseline
Establish a starting baseline for carbon, energy, water and waste before principal photography begins.
04
Production Data Collection
Daily logging of fuel, electricity, travel, accommodation, catering, water and waste across every shoot day — a running log, not a one-shot total.
05
Carbon & Resource Accounting
Activity data is converted to CO₂e using region-specific emission factors, alongside water and waste accounting.
06
Reduction Measures
Reduction measures are implemented and tracked against the live baseline as the production progresses.
07
Evidence Review
Supporting documentation is reviewed against logged activity — receipts, bills, records, declarations, and procurement data.
08
Climactix Assessment
Weighted scoring across eight dimensions produces the Climactix Production Sustainability Score.
09
Certification
Productions clearing a tier's minimum score and mandatory criteria are issued a signed, verifiable certificate.
10
Impact Report
A detailed report documents footprint, evidence coverage, certification outcome, and recommendations for the next production.

Certification Levels

Climactix Green Production certification.

These are Climactix Global certification levels, issued once a production clears a tier's minimum Climactix Production Sustainability Score and its mandatory evidence criteria. They do not imply government or third-party accreditation.

Bronze
40+ / 100
Baseline established
  • Carbon footprint measured
  • Waste segregation practiced
Establish baseline measurement across all five phases before targeting Silver.
Silver
55+ / 100
≤ 180 kg CO₂e / crew-day
  • + Water recycling program
  • + Local hiring priority
Reduce carbon intensity below the industry median and formalize a waste diversion plan.
Gold
70+ / 100
≤ 120 kg CO₂e / crew-day
  • + Renewable energy (partial)
  • + Single-use plastic ban
Reach 25%+ renewable energy share and 40%+ waste diversion to qualify for Platinum.
Platinum
82+ / 100
≤ 80 kg CO₂e / crew-day
  • + Renewable energy (majority)
  • + Biodiversity assessment
Offset residual emissions and formalize a biodiversity/community program to qualify for Diamond.
Diamond
92+ / 100
≤ 40 kg CO₂e / crew-day
  • + Residual emissions offset
  • + Community impact program
Maintain performance and publish a verified year-over-year improvement plan.

Climactix Production Sustainability Score™

A weighted 0–100 composite.

Each dimension blends quantitative activity data with qualitative questionnaire responses. Weighting is calibrated per production type and evolves as the underlying methodology matures.

Carbon
30%
Waste
15%
Energy
15%
Water
10%
Procurement
10%
Transportation
10%
Biodiversity
5%
Social
5%

How dimensions are scored. Carbon, water, transportation and waste blend logged activity data with questionnaire responses; energy, procurement, biodiversity and social factors are assessed primarily through structured qualitative review.

Carbon intensity (kg CO₂e per crew-day) is the single largest lever in the composite score, and is the same metric that anchors certification-level eligibility above.
